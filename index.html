Chapter I · The Lexicon

Words that build
a person

Every great result has a vocabulary behind it. These are the words people repeat to themselves at 5 a.m., defined plainly, with how to use them in your own life.

obsession

/əbˈsɛʃ.ən/ noun
  1. A goal or idea that fills your mind so completely that everything else is measured against it."Average people have hobbies. Builders have obsessions."
  2. Focus that no longer needs to be forced; you return to the work because you want to, not because you must.

discipline

/ˈdɪs.ə.plɪn/ noun
  1. The practice of doing what you planned to do, even when you don't feel like doing it.Motivation is a mood. Discipline is a decision.
  2. Training that shapes behavior until the right choice becomes the default one.

The 1% rule · 365 days

1% better vs 1% worse, every day

Improve by 1% each day for a year and you end up about 37.8 times better. Slip by 1% each day and you are left with 3% of where you started. The daily difference looks tiny; the yearly difference is everything.
